# Awesome-Sales-Prospecting

## Top Sales Prospecting Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on B2B Contact Data, Company Intelligence, Email/Phone Finder, Lead Enrichment & Outbound Prospecting*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Sales Prospecting**. These tools help sales and GTM teams find decision-makers, enrich contact and company data, build target lists, and accelerate outbound outreach.



**Examples** include Apollo.io, ZoomInfo, Lusha, Cognism, Kaspr, Snov.io, Uplead, LeadIQ, Seamless.AI, and RocketReach (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: True open-source equivalents to large commercial B2B databases do not exist — the core value is proprietary data. Useful open projects focus on **scraping, local lead discovery, enrichment scripts, and AI research agents**. This section lists the strongest available options and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Building targeted lists from public sources (Google Maps, company websites, open registries) with open scrapers and scoring.

- Using open email-verification and pattern tools to improve deliverability of self-sourced lists.

- Running local AI research agents to deepen account and contact understanding before outreach.

- Combining open discovery with commercial enrichment only where accuracy is critical.

- Accepting that large, continuously updated, verified global B2B databases remain the core strength of commercial platforms.

- Focusing open efforts on niche, local, or highly specific vertical prospecting where public data is sufficient.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Discover leads from public data → enrich with open scripts and free APIs → verify emails → score and prioritize → push to CRM or sequencer. This works well for local businesses, specific niches, or early-stage teams. Commercial platforms (Apollo, ZoomInfo, Lusha, Cognism, RocketReach, etc.) remain essential when you need broad, verified, frequently updated contact and company data at scale.
